Privacy Policy

✅ No personal data sold or shared

This Privacy Policy explains how Fidel: Learn Tigrinya ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the app. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

📋 Information We Collect

Fidel: Learn Tigrinya is a language-learning app. We collect minimal information to operate the service:

  • Usage data: Anonymous analytics such as which lessons are viewed, app open frequency, and feature interactions. This data cannot be used to identify you.
  • Crash reports: If the app crashes, a non-identifying diagnostic report may be generated to help us fix bugs.
  • Account information (if you create an account): Your display name and email address, used solely to save your progress and sync across devices.

We do not collect your location, contacts, camera images, microphone recordings, or any sensitive personal information.

🔊 Audio & Microphone

The App plays audio pronunciations of Tigrinya letters and words to help you learn. This audio is pre-recorded and bundled with the app — it does not use your device's microphone.

We do not record, store, or transmit any audio from your device.

🍪 Cookies & Local Storage

The App uses browser local storage to remember your progress (completed lessons, daily challenge streaks). This data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers unless you are signed in to a user account.

🔗 Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): Hosts the app and, if you create an account, stores your progress data securely. Firebase's privacy policy is available at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third parties.

👶 Children's Privacy

Fidel: Learn Tigrinya is designed to be family-friendly and is appropriate for all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App does not display ads and does not include in-app purchases.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

🔒 Data Security

Any data we store is protected using industry-standard encryption and access controls provided by Firebase/Google Cloud. We regularly review our security practices to ensure your information remains safe.

🌍 Data Retention

Anonymous usage and crash data is retained for up to 12 months and then deleted. If you create an account, your progress data is retained until you delete your account. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

⚖️ Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Opt out of analytics data collection

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below.
